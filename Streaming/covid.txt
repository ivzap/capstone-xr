COVID-19, caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus, is a highly contagious respiratory illness that primarily spreads through respiratory droplets. The virus features spike proteins on its surface that enable it to enter human cells by binding to the ACE2 receptor. Once inside, the virus replicates, leading to symptoms ranging from mild cold-like effects to severe pneumonia. COVID-19 can lead to complications such as acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), and it has impacted global health systems significantly, resulting in widespread infections and mortality
